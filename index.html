How the recommendations work

First, candidates must share a city and satisfy each other's age ranges. Then we rank the eligible profiles by cosine similarity of interest vectors. The cards show which interests contributed to the score.

This is a discovery prototype. It has no accounts, real users, messaging, or collection of personal information. Production use would require consent, privacy controls, blocking and reporting, moderation, and substantial evaluation with real users.
